About & Status

How SERENE-AI works

A short, honest description of what each feature does.

What it does

  • Chat: measures the tone of your message (positive, neutral or negative) and picks a prepared, supportive reply. It does not understand you the way a person does.
  • Habit Coach: follows simple, readable rules based on your four answers and tells you which rule applied.
  • Music: a playlist of songs tagged with your chosen mood, in random order.
  • Writing: gives a prompt for your mood. Your notes are not analysed or stored.
  • Habit Clustering: groups habits by the time they need and lists the ones that fit your free time.

Please remember

SERENE-AI is a self-care companion for learning and reflection. It is not a medical device, it cannot diagnose or treat anything, and it has not been clinically validated.

If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a qualified professional. If you might be in danger, contact your local emergency number.
